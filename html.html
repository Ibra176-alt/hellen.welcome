Your Projects
What you'll be working on
PROJECT 1

Agent Performance Scorecard

Every Week
What you'll do
Build and update a weekly scorecard for every village agent. Track five things per agent: farmers enrolled (new vs. returning), total savings collected in TZS, percentage of assigned farmers who made at least one payment that week, deliveries confirmed, and number of dropouts. Rank agents against each other. Flag the top 3 and bottom 3 every week.
Why it matters
Our agents are the face of HERVeg in the villages. If an agent is struggling, farmers suffer. If an agent is great, we need to know why and replicate it. This scorecard is how we hold our field network accountable without guessing.
What success looks like
The scorecard is delivered every Monday morning before the team meeting. It's clean, accurate, and takes you no more than 2 hours to produce. Within 4 weeks, the field team is using it to make real decisions — reassigning farmers, coaching struggling agents, recognizing top performers. If someone asks "how is Agent X doing?" you can answer in 30 seconds.
PROJECT 2

Savings Funnel Tracker

Every Week
What you'll do
Track every farmer's position in their savings journey. Payment 1, payment 2, payment 3, completed, or dropped. Each week, show how many farmers moved forward, how many stalled, and how many dropped out. Identify the exact payment stage where the biggest drop happens.
Why it matters
Our entire model runs on savings. If farmers don't complete their payments, they don't get inputs, and the whole cycle breaks. This tracker tells us exactly where the savings journey breaks so we can intervene at the right moment — through SMS nudges, agent visits, or adjusting the payment schedule.
What success looks like
Every Friday, you deliver a clear one-page snapshot showing farmer movement through the funnel. You can tell us "most farmers stall between payment 2 and 3 in Mtwara, but not in Njombe" — and suggest a reason. Within 6 weeks, your funnel data directly informs when and where our Amara SMS nudges fire.
PROJECT 3

Season-Over-Season Comparison

End of Each Season
What you'll do
After each season closes, build a side-by-side comparison of this season vs. last season. Cover: total farmers enrolled, new vs. returning, savings completion rate, average savings per farmer, products ordered vs. delivered, dropout rate, agent count and average load, revenue collected, and all of this split by Njombe and Mtwara. Add a % change column so the direction is obvious at a glance.
Why it matters
Without this, we're flying blind. We need to know — are we improving or not? This is the report that tells us the truth. It's also the backbone of every funder update and investor conversation. When someone asks "how's HERVeg doing?" this report is the answer.
What success looks like
Delivered within 2 weeks of the season closing — no exceptions. It includes a short written analysis (half a page max) with three sections: what improved and why, what got worse and why, what stayed flat and whether that's okay. Any funder could read it in 5 minutes and understand exactly where HERVeg stands.
PROJECT 4

Next Season Forecast

4 Weeks Before Each New Season
What you'll do
Using the season comparison data, build a forecast for the upcoming season under three scenarios. Conservative (same growth, same dropout — our floor), Base Case (modest improvements from specific actions we're taking), and Stretch (everything goes right — our ceiling). For each scenario, project: farmer enrollment, savings mobilized, product orders, revenue, agent capacity needed, and input procurement budget. Every assumption must be written down explicitly — no hidden numbers.
Why it matters
This is how we stop being reactive and start planning. If we know what to expect, we can procure the right amount of inputs, hire the right number of agents, and set realistic fundraising targets. It also shows funders that HERVeg thinks ahead — which builds trust and credibility.
What success looks like
Delivered 4 weeks before the new season starts. It fits on one page plus a half-page assumptions list. Ibrahim or any team lead can challenge any number by asking "what's behind that?" and you can answer immediately. By the second season you do this, you can compare your forecast to what actually happened — and improve your accuracy.
PROJECT 5

Grant Application Support

3 Submissions Per Week
What you'll do
Each week, Ibrahim will share grant application documents and target opportunities with you. Your job is to support the submission process — formatting documents, pulling the right data points from your trackers and reports, ensuring numbers are accurate and current, and helping get at least 3 grant applications submitted per week. You won't write the grants from scratch, but you'll make sure every submission has clean, accurate, ready-to-use data behind it.
Why it matters
Grants are fuel for HERVeg's growth. Every missed deadline or sloppy number in an application is a lost opportunity. Your data work across the other four projects directly feeds into this — the funder-ready numbers, the seasonal comparisons, the forecasts. This is where your analysis turns into real money for the organization.
What success looks like
3 grants submitted every week, on time, with accurate data. When a grant asks "how many farmers have you served?" or "what is your repayment rate?" — the answer is already in your spreadsheets, not something you scramble to calculate. Zero data errors in any submitted application.
